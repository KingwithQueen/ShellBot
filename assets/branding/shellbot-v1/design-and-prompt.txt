ShellBot 图标设计 v1

设计概念：待接入的躯壳。
银白色机械头壳代表本机的工具与执行能力。
没有眼睛、嘴和表情的中空面罩代表躯壳自身没有思想。

最终生成提示词（原文）：

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: finished app icon symbol for ShellBot, a native Windows application, suitable for desktop shortcut and system tray. One single original icon, not a presentation sheet.
Primary request: translate the idea of a soulless robotic shell operated by an intelligence located on another device into a beautifully simple, distinctive icon. The local robot is only the empty capable body; thought comes from outside.
Subject: a front-facing hollow robot head housing. Its primary form is a bold continuous rounded angular shell, a broad helmet-like upper shape, sturdy short side housings, and a clean tapered chin. A large dark recessed empty face opening is the dominant interior shape. It must be unmistakably a mechanical EMPTY SHELL, with no eyes, no mouth, no face, no little person inside, and no brain.
At the very top center, interrupt the outer housing with one neat receiving notch. A short detached cool signal-colored vertical bar is precisely aligned just above this notch, suggesting an external controller connecting into the shell. Make that simple separated bar and notch part of the strong silhouette, not an antenna, not Wi-Fi waves. The shell stays hollow.
Style: refined minimal geometric industrial brand icon, vector-like precision, confident thick shapes, carefully balanced negative space, crisp edges, only restrained bevel shading for material clarity. Neutral light machined shell around a deep graphite interior, and a single restrained cool cyan signal accent on the receiving bar/notch. No highly detailed metal textures.
Composition: centered, symmetrical, nearly fills a square canvas with 10 percent clear padding, isolated on genuinely transparent background. Strong contrast, readable simplified shape at 32 pixels. The shell and dark cavity form one complete icon and the small detached signal bar is the sole extra element.
Mood: capable, calm, impersonal, engineered. No threatening or cute character expression.
Constraints: NO text, NO letters, NO wordmark, NO watermark, NO mockup, NO rounded-square background tile, NO ground shadow, NO glow haze, NO circuitry ornament, NO clouds, NO cables, NO shellfish or snail, NO skull, NO cartoon eyes, NO full body. Deliver only the finished isolated icon at high resolution, genuine alpha transparency.
